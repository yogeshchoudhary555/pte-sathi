📋 Study Guide — kaunsa word kahan aayega

Describe Image

The [chart type] shows [topic]. The main point is that [sabse bada point + figure]. In addition, [doosra point + figure]. Overall, this suggests that [conclusion].

Retell Lecture

The lecture was about [topic]. First, the speaker explained that [point 1]. Then, the speaker discussed [point 2]. In conclusion, the speaker suggested that [conclusion].

Summarize Written Text (sirf 1 sentence)

[Main idea], because [point 1], and [point 2].

Essay (200-300 words)

Intro: [topic + apni position + 2 reasons] → Body 1: [reason 1 + explain + example] → Body 2: [reason 2 + explain + example] → Conclusion: [dobara position, short me]

Summarize Spoken Text (50-70 words)

[Topic] + [point 1] + [point 2 / example] + [conclusion], 3-4 sentences me.

Respond to a Situation (40 sec)

[Problem acknowledge karo] → [clear request] → [short reason] → Thank you, I would really appreciate your help.

Summarize Group Discussion (2 min)

The group discussed [topic]. One speaker argued that [point 1], while another pointed out that [point 2]. Overall, the group agreed that [agreement].
